What it does

Real-time BPM & Key

Detects tempo and musical key live from any tab using Essentia WASM and TempoCNN — runs 100% on your device.

Audio capture & export

Record up to 5 minutes from a tab and export to WAV. Every recording is auto-tagged with BPM and Key.

Source separation

Optional. Split a recording into vocals, drums, bass, and other stems using the Demucs model.

Local library

Searchable BPM/Key tagged library, stored only on your device via chrome.storage.local.
